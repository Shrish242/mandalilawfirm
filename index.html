FAQ

Common questions

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Bring your citizenship certificate, any relevant papers (land documents, agreements, notices, court orders) and a short written timeline of events.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. Everything you share with us is treated as strictly confidential.

Do you handle both civil and criminal cases?

Yes. We handle civil matters such as land, family and contract disputes, and criminal matters at every stage.

Can you give advice before I file a case?

Yes. Many problems can be understood, and sometimes resolved, with early legal advice.

How long will my case take?

It depends on the type and complexity of the case and the court. After reviewing your matter, we will give you a realistic expectation.
